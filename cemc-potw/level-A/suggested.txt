SUGGESTED CEMC PROBLEMS OF THE WEEK (Problem A, Grade 3/4)
Picked 2026-11-17 to match what Deniz and Toprak got wrong on the Math Kangaroo exams
(see QUESTIONS.md). Pages are PDF page numbers of the problem; the solution is on the next page(s).
Files: POTW-A_<year>_problems-and-solutions.pdf in this folder.

The pattern in the Kangaroo mistakes: almost never the arithmetic. They stop at the first answer that
looks right instead of (1) seeing shapes in their head, (2) listing every case, (3) finding hidden
numbers step by step, (4) checking every clue. The CEMC problems below train exactly these.
Most CEMC problems are money, data and measuring - those are skipped (no help for the weak spots).

Suggested order: one problem a day, mixing the four groups; do it on paper, then read the solution.
* = start with these (closest to Kangaroo questions they missed).


1. FINDING HIDDEN NUMBERS STEP BY STEP  (Kangaroo: balance scales, colour cards, hiding digits)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 2025-26  p21   Shape Sums             - symbols hide numbers, row/column sums given. Almost the same
                                          as Kangaroo 2022 Q24 (colour cards).
* 2022-23  p10   Balancing Act          - heart, moon, sun boxes on balances.
* 2023-24  p13   Balancing Act          - a new one with the same idea.
  2024-25  p6    Balancing Act          - a third one.
  2024-25  p103  Can You Kayak?         - kayaks + rowboats seats: compare two facts.
  2025-26  p8    Gathering Flowers      - five kinds of flowers, chain of "more than" clues.
  2022-23  p22   The Pencil Case        - riddle with remainders: guess and check, all clues.
  2025-26  p128  Sorting Socks          - remainders again, "fewer than 60", find the largest.
  2023-24  p117  What's in the Pouch?   - $20.30 in exactly 4 coins and 3 bills.


2. CHECKING EVERY CLUE  (Kangaroo: rule out cards, logic clues, fewest shapes, one guess is right)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 2025-26  p44   Tents Situation        - logic grid: families, sizes, tent colours, "not" clues.
* 2022-23  p117  Track and Field        - logic grid: who has which nickname.
  2024-25  p19   Entry Code             - test 8 codes against 4 rules: every rule, every code.
  2022-23  p18   What Number Am I?      - digit clues (3-digit).
  2023-24  p21   What Number Am I?      - digit clues (4-digit).
  2024-25  p17   What Number Am I?      - sum 11, product 16, no repeats.
  2022-23  p109  Finding Treasure       - rooms, doors and keys: fewest keys to reach the treasure.


3. LISTING EVERY CASE IN ORDER  (Kangaroo: seating orders, routes, counting with a tree, two patterns)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 2024-25  p37   Summer Selections      - shorts, shirts, hats, all different colours: list them all.
* 2025-26  p15   Team Tournament        - how many matches when every team plays every team.
* 2025-26  p41   Park Paths             - trails between two stations: compare routes, find shorter.
  2023-24  p48   Rearranging Rectangles - draw ALL rectangles from 15, 24, 17 tiles.
  2023-24  p107  Patterned Savings      - every day / every 2nd / every 5th day: two patterns at once.
  2025-26  p37   Just Filling In        - arrange 6 digits; how many different solutions?
  2024-25  p70   Calculator Catastrophe - make 1 to 12 with broken buttons (like "make a target number").
  2024-25  p51   Fencepost Problem      - posts and gaps: the classic "one more / one less" trap.


4. SEEING SHAPES IN YOUR HEAD  (Kangaroo: cube counting, folding, pieces that fit, perimeter, ant path)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 2024-25  p15   Stacking Shapes        - shapes on top of each other: in what order were they laid?
* 2025-26  p80   Paper Folding          - fold a square corner to corner; predict the shapes. Use real paper!
* 2022-23  p65   What's My Perimeter?   - square cut into rectangles (like Kangaroo 2002 Q6).
  2024-25  p54   Quilting Puzzle        - squares inside a rectangle: find the missing lengths.
  2025-26  p10   Puzzling Predicament   - 100-piece puzzle: how many edge pieces? (like hidden cubes inside)
  2024-25  p22   Amazing Navigation     - maze through every white square exactly once.
  2022-23  p57   Tracking Triangles     - cut polygons into triangles; find the pattern.
  2022-23  p54   Star of Lakshmi        - two squares, one turned: sides, corners, symmetry.

Not enough spatial practice here (CEMC Problem A has few 3-D problems): for cube counting and
folding, also build with snap cubes and fold real paper, and use the website's cube questions.
