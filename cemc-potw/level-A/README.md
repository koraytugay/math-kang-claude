# CEMC Problem of the Week - Problem A (Grade 3/4)

Free weekly problems from the University of Waterloo's Centre for Education in Mathematics and
Computing (CEMC): https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/resources/potw (archive:
https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/resources/potw-archive). Downloaded 2026-11-17 for the kids' practice.

A problem with two themes is printed twice (once in each theme's section), so the booklets
have 40-47 problem pages but 30 different problems each. Each problem page shows the problem's themes (Number Sense, Algebra, Geometry & Measurement,
Data Management, Computational Thinking) in the corner.

`suggested.txt` lists the problems that match the kids' Math Kangaroo weak spots.

Not here: English booklets before 2022-23 are no longer on the CEMC site (only French copies
of 2021-22 exist), and this year's weeks 1-7 are no longer linked. The CEMC posts one new
problem each week and its solution a week later; a full 2026-27 booklet should appear in the
archive after the school year.
